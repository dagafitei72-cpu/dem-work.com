Questions

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

The installers are new and not yet signed with a code-signing certificate, so SmartScreen does not know them yet. Press “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Every file is published only on this site’s GitHub page and scanned before release.

Are they really free?

Yes. Both are freeware by Dandem: free to use at home and at work. No accounts, no ads, nothing to subscribe to.

How do I get new versions?

DemVid looks for updates by itself and installs them when you restart it. For ArDem, download the new installer from this page and run it: your servers and the vault stay as they are.

Where do my machines, servers and passwords go?

Nowhere. Everything stays on your PC: virtual machines in a folder you choose, servers and the encrypted vault in your Windows profile. The apps only go online to fetch updates (DemVid) or to reach your own servers (ArDem).

What open-source parts do they use?

DemVid runs its machines with QEMU; ArDem builds on xterm.js, CodeMirror and ssh2. Licenses come with each app, and the source of QEMU and its firmware is attached to every DemVid release.
